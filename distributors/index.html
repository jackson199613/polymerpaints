Distributor problems

What we help you solve.

  1. 01
    End-user colour demand is scattered, stock is a burden and powder has a shelf life.
  2. 02
    Unstable supplier lead times cause stock-outs and broken promises to end users.
  3. 03
    Without texture, metallic and certified products, higher-value orders are lost.
  4. 04
    A new supplier’s batch consistency is unknown, and complaints are costly.
  5. 05
    Price competition is intense; low price alone does not keep end users.

Working together

Four steps, from pack to repeat orders.

  1. 01
    Starter pack. Catalogue, certificates, colour cards, panels and application limits, so you can screen end users without over-promising.
  2. 02
    End-user screening. A list of applicators, aluminium, furniture, cabinet and appliance makers with coating lines and colour needs.
  3. 03
    Sample trials. You bring the part and colour need; we match and support the trial so each project can be verified.
  4. 04
    Repeat orders. Managed by colour, batch and customer ownership. We start non-exclusive and discuss regional terms on results.
