Questions

Before you specify.

Which powder coating system should I use for outdoor parts and which for indoor parts?

Use polyester for outdoor and UV-exposed parts. Use epoxy-polyester hybrid for indoor decorative parts and epoxy for indoor functional parts that need chemical resistance or hardness.

POLYMER supplies three thermosetting systems, and the choice is driven mainly by where the coated part will be used. Polyester is the weather-resistant system in our catalogue and is the one we recommend for architectural aluminium, outdoor furniture and lighting, air-conditioner housings, outdoor cabinets and agricultural machinery.

The epoxy-polyester hybrid gives good flow and gloss and is intended for indoor items such as appliances, steel furniture, shelving and indoor enclosures. Epoxy offers good chemical resistance, hardness and adhesion, and suits indoor functional parts such as safes, security doors, instrument housings and fitness equipment.

Neither epoxy nor hybrid should be specified for outdoor use. If one product range contains both indoor and outdoor parts, select the system part by part rather than using one powder for everything. Final suitability is confirmed on your parts, with your pretreatment and cure conditions.

Basis: POLYMER product catalogue (system descriptions and application areas).

What does Qualicoat Class 1 mean, and which POLYMER products does it cover?

Qualicoat Class 1 is the standard-durability approval level for powder coatings on architectural aluminium. At POLYMER it covers one polyester system, PLM100611, under licence P-2199.

Qualicoat is a quality label for coatings on aluminium used in architecture. Its Class 1 level is the standard-durability category. Higher durability classes exist, and POLYMER does not hold them.

The approval applies to our polyester system PLM100611, licence P-2199, gloss category 3. It does not include structured-texture or metallic extensions, and it does not extend to our other polyester products, to epoxy or hybrid powders, or to every colour we make. The current certificate is valid until 31 December 2026 and will be updated when renewed.

If your project requires a Qualicoat-approved powder, tell us the colour, gloss and finish so we can confirm whether it falls within the approved system. If a specification calls for a higher Qualicoat class, that is outside our current approval.

Basis: POLYMER Qualicoat certificate (licence P-2199) as stated in company records.
