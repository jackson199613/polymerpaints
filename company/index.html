What we do

The right system, the matched colour, on time.

We produce three powder systems: epoxy for indoor functional parts, polyester for outdoor parts, and epoxy-polyester hybrid for indoor decorative parts. Our polyester system PLM100611 is Qualicoat Class 1 approved.

Our work starts from the customer’s part and where it will be used. We recommend the system, match the colour and finish, and record the approved panel so every batch can be checked against it. Standard lead time is within 10 days, and we do not make low-end powder.

Environment

Powder, by nature.

Powder coatings are applied as a dry powder and contain no solvent carrier, and oversprayed powder can be reclaimed and reused on many lines. Our environmental management system is certified to ISO 14001.

We make no further environmental claims without data. If your purchasing team needs specific declarations, ask us and we will confirm what can be supplied for the product concerned.
