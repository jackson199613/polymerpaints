Three product systems

Chosen by where the part will live.

Outdoor or indoor, decorative or functional. The environment decides the chemistry; the panel confirms it.

Outdoor

PolyesterQualicoat

Weather-resistant, resists yellowing, strong edge coverage and impact strength.

Typical partsAluminium profiles, outdoor furniture and lighting, AC housings, farm machinery

Polyester range
Indoor · decorative

Hybrid

Epoxy-polyester with good flow and high gloss, for appearance-led indoor metal.

Hybrid range

Epoxy

Chemical resistance, electrical insulation, hardness and strong adhesion.

Typical partsSecurity doors, safes, electrical cabinets, industrial and fitness equipment

Epoxy range
