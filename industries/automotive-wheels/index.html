Automotive parts & wheels.

Confirm the specification and coating system first, then evaluate parts and wheels under clear technical conditions.

Main: PolyesterSelective project
Request a sample Talk to us

Who this is for

Automotive accessories, wheels, bicycles.

Our catalogue lists automotive accessories and bicycles as polyester applications, so we keep automotive parts and wheels as selective projects. Wheels and automotive parts usually have a complete coating system, defined specifications and a supplier approval process, so suitability cannot be judged from a colour panel alone. We confirm the specification first, evaluate the product second, and arrange samples last.

Buyer problems

What usually goes wrong.

  1. 01
    Wheel coatings usually combine primer, colour and clear layers that must work together.
  2. 02
    Customers have defined specifications, tests and approval processes.
  3. 03
    Colour and metallic effects must be consistent, because batches show on the finished vehicle.
  4. 04
    Corrosion, stone-chip and chemical resistance requirements differ by customer.
