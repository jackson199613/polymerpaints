Who this is for

Steel furniture, shelving and racking, office products, security doors, safes, household metalware, lighting, outdoor furniture.

Metal furniture, shelving and household metalware use powder steadily, in many colours, with frequent repeats. Buyers care most that doors, frames, shelves and small parts match, that large panels flow well, and that colour changes are quick. Indoor furniture, shelving and lighting suit hybrid; security doors, safes and office products that need more wear and corrosion resistance can use epoxy; outdoor furniture and lighting use polyester.

Buyer problems

What usually goes wrong.

  1. 01
    Colour, gloss and texture that differ between doors, frames, shelves and small parts.
  2. 02
    Flow, orange peel and edge coverage on large flat panels.
  3. 03
    Many colours and frequent changes; slow sampling delays new products and orders.
  4. 04
    The same range often has indoor and outdoor parts that need different systems.
  5. 05
    Balancing appearance, transfer efficiency and cost.
