Electrical & new-energy enclosures.

Hybrid indoors and polyester outdoors, with steady supply of standard cabinet colours.

Main: Hybrid, PolyesterOption: Epoxy
Request a sample Talk to us

Who this is for

Distribution boxes, control and switch cabinets, outdoor electrical boxes, instrument housings, inverter, battery storage and UPS housings.

Enclosures usually run in a few standard colours such as RAL 7035, so buyers value colour stability over large volumes, an even sand texture, and coverage on punched holes and folded edges. Indoor cabinets and instrument housings suit hybrid; outdoor cabinets suit polyester; indoor parts that need chemical resistance or electrical insulation can use epoxy.

Buyer problems

What usually goes wrong.

  1. 01
    Colour and gloss drift between batches on standard colours.
  2. 02
    Even sand texture that also hides marks.
  3. 03
    Coverage and film on punched holes, folded edges and welds.
  4. 04
    Weathering and corrosion of outdoor cabinets in heat, humidity and coastal air.
  5. 05
    New-energy projects ask for documents and tests.
