Agricultural & industrial equipment.

Polyester outdoors and epoxy or hybrid indoors, so brand colours stay stable and durable.

Main: PolyesterOption: Epoxy, Hybrid
Request a sample Talk to us

Who this is for

Farm machinery and implements, industrial equipment covers and guards, fitness equipment, valves and pipe.

Machinery faces appearance demands, mechanical wear, sunlight and contact with media at the same time. Outdoor parts such as farm machinery, valves and pipe suit polyester; indoor industrial and fitness equipment can use epoxy for hardness and wear or hybrid for appearance. Heavy-duty anti-corrosion and immersion parts need dedicated systems and are outside this general recommendation.

Buyer problems

What usually goes wrong.

  1. 01
    Matching brand colours such as machinery red, green or yellow, batch after batch.
  2. 02
    Fading and gloss loss in sun, and contact with fertilisers and agrochemicals.
  3. 03
    Coverage and cure on welds, thick sections and complex frames.
  4. 04
    Impact, scratching and wear during handling, assembly and use.
  5. 05
    One powder for the whole machine leaves some parts over- or under-protected.
