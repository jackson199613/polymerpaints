Privacy & cookies.

How we handle the details you send us through this website. Last updated 27 October 2026.

This notice is provided by Fujian Polymer Industry & Trade Co., Ltd., No. 2 Zhuli Road, Xiangcheng District, Zhangzhou, Fujian, China (“POLYMER”, “we”). It explains what personal data we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit a form we receive the details you enter: name, company, work email, country or region, role, phone, shipping address for samples, and the content of your message. We do not ask for sensitive personal data.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request, prepare samples, documents or quotations, and follow up on your project (steps taken at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries).
  • To keep records of business correspondence as required by law.

We do not sell your data or use it for third-party marketing.

Who processes it

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., which also receives form submissions on our behalf. Data may therefore be processed outside your country, including in the United States and China, with appropriate safeguards where required.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to business are deleted within 24 months. Records linked to orders are kept as long as accounting and legal rules require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or receive a copy. You may also complain to your data protection authority. To exercise a right, email sales@polymerpaints.com, use the contact form or call +86 596 2600611.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across sites. Fonts and images are served from our own site. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this notice and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Changes

We may update this notice; the date above shows the latest version.

This notice is provided for transparency and should be reviewed by legal counsel for your target markets.
