How to store and handle powder coatings.

Keep powder cool, dry and sealed, rotate stock, and follow the SDS for safe handling.

When you need this

You hold stock as a distributor or applicator, or your warehouse is hot or humid. Powder is a reactive product: poor storage can cause clumping, poor fluidisation, uneven film build and changes in application or cure behaviour that only show up on the line.

Related questions

  • When should I consider a low-cure powder coating?
  • How does POLYMER work with distributors and importers?

Steps

  1. Store cool and dry

    Keep powder in a dry, well-ventilated area away from heat sources and direct sun. Follow the storage temperature on the TDS.

  2. Keep containers sealed

    Close containers after use to avoid moisture pick-up and contamination.

  3. Avoid condensation

    If powder has been stored cold, let the closed container reach room temperature before opening.

  4. Rotate stock

    Use first-in, first-out and check shelf life on the TDS. Pay closer attention to low-cure grades.

  5. Check before use

    Make sure powder flows freely and fluidises well. Do not use powder with hard lumps.

  6. Handle safely

    Follow the SDS: avoid dust clouds and ignition sources, ground equipment and use suitable personal protection.

Common mistakes

  • Storing powder near ovens or under a hot roof.
  • Opening cold containers in a warm, humid room.
  • Leaving partly used containers open.
  • Holding low-cure stock longer than the TDS allows.
  • Mixing powder from different products or batches in one container.
