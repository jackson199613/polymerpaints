How to choose between epoxy, polyester and hybrid powder coatings.

Pick the powder system from the part's service environment first, then refine by appearance and functional needs.

When you need this

You are specifying a powder for a new part, consolidating suppliers, or your product range mixes indoor and outdoor parts. Choosing the system before colour avoids costly field failures such as chalking or discoloration outdoors.

Related questions

  • Which powder coating system should I use for outdoor parts and which for indoor parts?
  • Can epoxy-polyester hybrid powder be used outdoors?
  • What does Qualicoat Class 1 mean, and which POLYMER products does it cover?

Steps

  1. Define the environment

    Decide whether each part will see sunlight or weather at any point in its life. Any UV exposure points to polyester.

  2. Separate indoor decorative from indoor functional

    For indoor parts where flow, gloss and appearance matter most, such as appliances and furniture, start with hybrid. Where chemical resistance, hardness or abrasion resistance matter more, consider epoxy.

  3. Check special requirements

    Note whether the project needs Qualicoat approval, a specific test standard or customer approval. Only POLYMER polyester system PLM100611 is Qualicoat Class 1 approved.

  4. Choose cure grade

    Select standard cure, or low cure if your oven, part mass or energy targets call for it. Confirm the cure schedule on the TDS.

  5. Add colour, gloss and effect

    Select the RAL or custom colour, the gloss band and any texture or metallic effect within the chosen system.

  6. Verify on real parts

    Run a panel or line trial with your pretreatment and oven before committing to production.

Common mistakes

  • Using one powder for a whole product range that has both indoor and outdoor parts.
  • Specifying hybrid outdoors because it performs well indoors.
  • Assuming every polyester colour or texture is covered by a Qualicoat approval.
  • Treating indoor epoxy powder as a pipeline or heavy-duty anticorrosion coating.
